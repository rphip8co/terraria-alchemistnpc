## 📖 About

🔹 **Terraria — AlchemistNPC** is a powerful tool built to help you purchase potions and alchemical supplies from the Alchemist NPC in your Terraria town. The Alchemist NPC sells buffing potions, materials, and special brews that become available as you progress through the game.

🔸 Whether you're a seasoned professional or just getting started, **Terraria — AlchemistNPC** delivers a clean, intuitive experience packed with everything you need — right out of the box. Join thousands of users who've already streamlined their Terraria modding workflow. Download now and get started in minutes. 🚀

## 🚀 Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Potion Shop | Buy all vanilla buff potions from the Alchemist NPC |
| Material Sales | Purchase rare alchemical ingredients without grinding drops |
| Progression Gated | Item availability expands as you defeat bosses and explore biomes |
| Custom Brews | Unique NPC-exclusive potions with effects not found in vanilla crafting |
